## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you are a security researcher or a volunteer and you have found a "glitch in the prism," we want to hear about it! Please help us keep our users safe by reporting vulnerabilities responsibly.

### How to report:
  1. Private Disclosure: Please DO NOT open a public issue. We want to fix the leak before the ship sinks!
  2. Contact: Send a detailed report to nguyentrongphuc30012011@gmail.com.
  3. What to include: * A clear description of the vulnerability.<br>
    - Steps to reproduce (PoC).<br>
    - Potential impact on the user's data or browser.

## Our Promise
We value the contribution of the security community. We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and work closely with you to roll out a patch for the v3.9.x branch as quickly as possible.
